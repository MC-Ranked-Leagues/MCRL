---
title: How are the time slots decided?
description: An overview of MCRL's structure.
---

We use a rotating system of time slots to give everyone a fair chance to play, regardless of where they are in the world. These are the times and the order in which they rotate, so if the time slot for this week is #3, next week will be #4. A higher league is always 1 step behind in the rotation, so when you promote, the next week will be at the same time. All the leagues are usually hosted in a single 24-hour period. The day and time may be changed if there's something else happening at the same time. Times for the upcoming week are announced in the league-specific channels.

| Slot | Day & Time (UTC) |
| ---- | ---------------- |
| #1   | Sunday 0         |
| #2   | Saturday 12      |
| #3   | Sunday 4         |
| #4   | Saturday 16      |
| #5   | Sunday 8         |
| #6   | Saturday 20      |
