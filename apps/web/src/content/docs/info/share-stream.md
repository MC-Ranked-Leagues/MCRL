---
title: Share your stream
description: How to share your perspective with the community.
---

We encourage all competitors to stream their matches! If you want to appear on the main stream, use `/reg streaming:true`. Save it first with `/twitch username`, or let the bot use your Twitch connection on MCSR Ranked. You need to opt in each week you register. Keep an eye open at the info channels for any potential announcements regarding streaming.
