The seed type distribution for each league is listed here. No buried treasure seeds in leagues 5 and 6. No bucket completion ruined portals in league 6. There will always be at least 1 of each included seed type.

| Leagues | Seeds | Ship / BT | Village / DT | RP  |
| ------- | ----- | --------- | ------------ | --- |
| 1, 2, 3 | 8     | 3         | 3            | 2   |
| 4       | 6     | 2         | 3            | 1   |
| 5, 6    | 5     | 1 (ship)  | 3            | 1   |
| 7       | 2     | 0         | 2            | 0   |

The seeds are checked before. So everything is intended. They are not guaranteed to be good or fun, just playable. There will be no seed changes.

We are using our own seed filter to make our job easier. It is meant to have parity with the ranked filter, however there may be minor differences. We're using automated evaluation to try to give a balance of easier and harder seeds.
