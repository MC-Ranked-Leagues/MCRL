We use a rotating system of time slots to give everyone a fair chance to play, regardless of where they are in the world. The slots rotate every week, so after A you get B, and so on. Times move to the higher leagues, so if league 3 has time C this week, league 2 will have it next week. That means when you promote, you play at the same time the next week. The day and time may be changed if there's something else happening at the same time. Times for the upcoming week are announced in the league-specific channels.
