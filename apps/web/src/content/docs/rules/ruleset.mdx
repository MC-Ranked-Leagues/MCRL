## 1. Joining Leagues

1. Players must use `/signup` in the [signup channel](https://discordapp.com/channels/1559925008116097196/1566236429443661965) in order to play. Players are required to link their Discord account with their MCSR Ranked account.
   - Use `/link` to learn how.
2. Players may only use multiple accounts with explicit permission.
3. Players may connect their Twitch account using `/twitch` if they want to be shown on mainstream.

## 2. Match Procedures

1. Times for the week will be posted a few days before the start of a match.
2. Players must use `/reg` in their league's channel in order to sign up.
   - Registration usually opens up an hour before the match begins.
   - If a player wants to be shown on mainstream, they may toggle streaming to true. Streaming guidelines can be found in [Player Conduct](#3-player-conduct). Note that if you are streaming but do not wish to be featured on mainstream, you do not need to mark streaming as true.
3. The host will send reminders when a seed is about to start, so make sure you are checking in between seeds.
   - If you need to be a bit late, ping the host and explain why. It is host discretion whether or not they delay.
4. There is a 10 minute break after half the seeds have finished. Make sure you're ready to rejoin the room after the break.

## 3. Player Conduct

1. Players must exhibit good sportsmanship at all times. This includes but is not limited to:
   - No trolling (e.g. spamming)
   - No toxicity (e.g. harassment)
   - Any use of racist, homophobic, or other discriminatory slurs will be met with an indefinite ban.
2. Players must follow fair play rules at all times. Any act that is intended to gain or give others an unfair advantage violates fair play. This includes but is not limited to:
   - Using cheats/illegal macros
   - Streamsniping (including mainstream)
   - Being in a call with another person who is feeding information
   - Spoiling seeds (discussing the seed with other players who are still playing during the seed)
   - Sandbagging (intentionally demoting below your skill level to play weaker opponents)
   - Any violations of fair play will be dealt with accordingly based on frequency and severity.
3. Players who are streaming and sign up with `streaming:true` must have a watchable stream.
   - Gameplay must be fully visible or feature only minor obstructions (facecam, Ninjabrain Bot, chat, etc).
   - The color and dimensions of said gameplay must not be distorted in any way that significantly impacts its visibility or viewing experience.
   - Mods have the final say when determining if a stream is watchable or not.
4. Players must follow the [MCSR Ranked TOS](https://mcsrranked.com/guidelines) and [Twitch TOS](https://legal.twitch.com/legal/terms-of-service/) (if streaming) at all times.
   - Note that being tournament banned from Ranked does not mean you are automatically banned from Leagues. Mods will deal with each player on a case by case basis.

## 4. Discord Conduct

1. Players must be respectful at all times.
   - Any use of slurs or hate speech will be met with an indefinite ban.
2. Players are prohibited from sending NSFW/inappropriate content.
3. Players must not spam.
4. Players must speak English only.
5. Players must follow the [Discord TOS](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

## 5. Use Common Sense

1. Moderators reserve the right to punish behavior not explicitly listed in this list based on its intent, severity, and impact.
