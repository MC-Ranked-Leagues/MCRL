---
title: How are points earned?
description: An overview of MSCL's scoring system.
---

Points for each placement are calculated using this formula, rounded down:

> _Points = (Players / 2) - (Placement - 1) + Bonus_

Where `Players` is the total number of players in the match, and `Placement` is your rank in the seed (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.). However you will always receive at least 1 point for completing a seed.

Additionally, the first three to finish a seed will get bonus points: first +5, second +3 and third +1.

For example, if there are 25 players in a match:

- 1st place: (25/2) - (1-1) + 5 = 17.5 ≈ 17 points
- 2nd place: (25/2) - (2-1) + 3 = 14.5 ≈ 14 points
- 3rd place: (25/2) - (3-1) + 1 = 11.5 ≈ 11 points
- 4th place: (25/2) - (4-1) + 0 = 9.5 ≈ 9 points
- 10th place: (25/2) - (10-1) + 0 = 12.5 - 9 + 0 = 3.5 ≈ 3 points
- 12th place and below: 1 point
- No completion: 0 points

Total points from all the seeds determine promotions and relegations.
